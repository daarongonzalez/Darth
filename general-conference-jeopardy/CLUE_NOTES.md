## Things to confirm

- **Titles:** Titles such as "Elder" and "Sister" are only used where the talks make them clear. Some answers (for example Sean Douglas and Paul H. Sinclair) have no title yet.
- **Answer wording:** The game shows answers in "What is…?" and "Who is…?" form. Say so if you would rather have plain answers.
- **Clue length:** The longest clues are about 200 characters. They fit on the card at desktop size, but test them on your projector or screen.
- **Difficulty:** The values reflect my guess at how hard each card is. A practice round would show which ones need to move.
